BELMONTE POS

Install on the till

For SUNMI T2 counter terminals. Takes about two minutes.

Download the app

Version 0.2.1 · 23.0 MB · Android 7.0 or newer

This is a test build It runs on sample products and does not connect to the Belmonte system. Sales are not recorded. It is for checking how the app feels on the terminal, and for testing the built-in printer — not for serving customers.

Installing

  1. Open this page on the terminal itself and tap Download the app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say installing from this source is blocked — tap Settings on that message.
  3. Turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

If the terminal has no browser

  1. Download the file on a computer and copy it to a USB stick.
  2. Plug the stick into the terminal and open the file manager.
  3. Tap the .apk file and follow the same steps above.

Testing the printer

  1. Sign in with any username and password, then pick a branch.
  2. Tap Test print at the top right of the sale screen.
  3. A report appears either way. Tap Share report to send it as text.

If nothing prints, the report says what the printer itself reported — send that rather than describing it.

What to look out for

  1. Does the barcode scanner add items without tapping the screen?
  2. Is the text readable while standing at the counter?
  3. Are the buttons easy to hit accurately?
  4. Does anything feel slow?
